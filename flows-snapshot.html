⚠ Redemption concentration — the finding that matters

The large block redemptions in both EarnETH and EarnUSD were executed by the same multisig (0x93a797643d…35d1). Outflow across the whole book is dominated by one counterparty, and when it moves, it hits both vaults at once.

Curation read: the redemption buffer and the SLA aren’t sized to a statistical distribution of retail withdrawals — they’re sized to this wallet. That argues for (1) knowing the holder and their redemption calendar, (2) a side-letter / notice period on tickets above a threshold, and (3) an LCR stress that assumes this entity exits both vaults in the same week. It also means headline TVL is softer than it looks: a large share can leave on one signature.

What this means for the buffer & SLA

Routine flow is a non-event. A typical settlement is $29.4k and the largest routine day was $865k — well under 1% of TVL. A same-day buffer of just 5% of TVL ($7.71M) covers 100+ typical settlement days. Retail redemptions never threaten this book.

The tail is what you size for. Block redemptions did $52.4M over 30 days — the biggest was $42.7M (27.7% of TVL) in a single settlement. The worst rolling 7 days was $43.2M. A policy stress wave of 20% in 7 days = $30.8M was actually exceeded last month (1.4×) — the tail scenario isn't hypothetical here, it happened, and the 48h SLA is what absorbed it. Size the LCR to this number, never to the median.

Current buffer: ~7% idle — inside the healthy band.

How this is measured no flow API exists — raw events

Source of truth. A deposit ends as a mint of the vault share token (Transfer from 0x0); a redemption ends as a burn (Transfer to 0x0). Both settle at an oracle report, so this is money that has actually left the queue and moved — not pending requests. Fee mints to the treasury (0xccf2…9b85) are excluded, so deposits are net of fees.
Deposits are granular; withdrawals batch. There are hundreds of small mint events (real per-deposit activity) but only ~30 burn events in a month — each burn is the redeem queue being flushed at a report. So a withdrawal “day” is a settlement day.
Routine vs block. Each settlement is classed routine (<1% of TVL) or a block redemption (≥1% of TVL). For every block redemption the settling transaction is fetched and its selector checked — 0x6a761202 is a Gnosis Safe execTransaction, i.e. a multisig-executed redemption. The Safe address is shown so you can see when the same counterparty hits both vaults.
Share tokens. EarnETH 0xBBFC…a0A4 · EarnUSD 0x4Ce1…1981. Logs pulled in ≤9,900-block chunks from Tenderly’s public gateway (drpc/blastapi fallback), day-mapped by measuring the real seconds-per-block across the window. Shares valued at the current share price ($2,044 / $1.40); month-long drift is <1% and doesn’t move the medians. A coverage figure flags any window that came back partial on free RPCs.
