07Methodology

Benchmark stack: tier 1 = passive baseline (stETH APR / sUSDS); tier 2 = market beta (TVL-weighted top vaults, vaults.fyi-style); tier 3 = named-peer median (this board). All APYs net-of-fees as reported by each source. Risk adjustment: spread / σ, where σ = standard deviation of daily APY over the last 90 days (computed here from the same histories) — note σ understates tail risk for credit and bridge exposures, so overlay the allocation flags.

Live sources: DefiLlama yields.llama.fi/chart/{pool} per pool · Mellow api.mellow.finance/v1/chain/1/core-vaults/{vault}/data + /timeweighted-apy · Lido eth-api.lido.fi/v1/protocol/steth/apr/last · Seven Seas api.sevenseas.capital. Row click-throughs fetch on demand: Morpho api.morpho.org/graphql (V1 + V2 vaults) · Yearn ydaemon.yearn.fi · Fluid Lite api.instadapp.io / api.fluid-lite.instadapp.ai. All fetched client-side; nothing stored, no keys.
