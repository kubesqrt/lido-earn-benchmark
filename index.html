Methodology

Benchmark stack: tier 1 = passive baseline (stETH APR / sUSDS); tier 2 = market beta (vaults.fyi-style TVL-weighted top vaults); tier 3 = named-peer median (this board). All APYs net-of-fees as reported by each source. Risk adjustment: spread / σ with σ = DefiLlama's APY standard deviation; note σ understates tail risk for credit and bridge exposures — overlay the allocation flags.

Live sources: DefiLlama yields.llama.fi/pools + /chart/{pool} · Mellow api.mellow.finance/v1/chain/1/core-vaults/{vault}/data + /timeweighted-apy · Lido eth-api.lido.fi/v1/protocol/steth/apr/last · Seven Seas api.sevenseas.capital. All fetched client-side; nothing is stored.
