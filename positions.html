00How leverage is worked out

L (leverage) = C / E = C / (C − D) · C = gross collateral, D = debt, E = your equity
max L = 1 / (1 − LTV) · e.g. 93% LTV ⇒ 14.3× theoretical max
net APY = (s·C − b·D) / E = s·L − b·(L−1) · s = yield on collateral, b = borrow rate
liquidation drawdown = 1 − LTVnow / LT · how far collateral price falls before liquidation

How we measured it: each position lives at an on-chain address. We read its aToken balance (= C) and variable-debt-token balance (= D) directly, so L is arithmetic, not an assumption. Worked example (E1): equity $47.0M, measured L 6.82× ⇒ C = 6.82 × 47.0 = $320M gross wstETH supplied, D = $273M WETH borrowed. Net = (2.21%×320 − 2.01%×273)/47 = ≈3.3% — matching the measured 3.27%. The loop "multiplies" the vault's capital: $47M of depositor money controls $320M of positions. That's also why unwinds move markets: the gross, not the equity, is what must exit.
