Settled deposits and redemptions for EarnETH and EarnUSD, read from share-token mint/burn events and bucketed by day. Deposits settle near-continuously; redemptions settle in batches at oracle reports — so outflow days are settlement days.

Reading the chain… first scan takes 10–25 seconds.

Method

Mint from 0x0 = settled deposit (fee mints to the treasury excluded). Burn to 0x0 = the redeem queue settling — settlements are batch transactions executed by vault operations, so they say nothing about who redeemed. Requester-level flow comes from share transfers into the queue; depositor-level from mint forwarding. Shares are valued at the current share price. Logs pulled client-side in ≤9,900-block chunks from public RPCs; a coverage figure appears if any range failed. Numbers exclude requests still pending in the queue.
